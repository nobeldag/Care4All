Simple Healthcare Terms

No confusing language. These are real healthcare terms explained simply.

Premium

The monthly amount you pay to keep your insurance active.

Deductible

The amount you pay for covered care before your insurance starts paying more of the cost.

Copay

A fixed amount you pay for a service, like a doctor visit or prescription.

Coinsurance

Your percentage share of a covered healthcare cost after your deductible.

Out-of-Pocket Maximum

The most you pay in a year for covered services before your plan pays 100% of covered costs.

Sliding Fee Scale

A reduced price based on your income and family size, often used by community health centers.

Best Value Suggestions

Care4All recommends starting with community health centers, official marketplaces, and local navigators before paying full price.

📍 If uninsured

Start with HRSA Find a Health Center, 211 Maryland, CCI Health Services, Mary’s Center, or Chesapeake Health Care.

⚖️ If comparing plans

Do not only look at the premium. Compare deductible, copay, coinsurance, and out-of-pocket maximum.

🔎 Search by need

Try searches like “dental no insurance,” “primary care sliding fee,” or “insurance enrollment help.”
